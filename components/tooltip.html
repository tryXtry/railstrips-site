Tooltip

Tooltips provide additional information on hover or focus.

Positions

Tooltip on top
Tooltip on bottom
Tooltip on left
Tooltip on right

Usage

<div data-controller="tooltip" class="relative inline-block">
  <button data-action="mouseenter->tooltip#show mouseleave->tooltip#hide focus->tooltip#show blur->tooltip#hide">
    Hover me
  </button>
  
  <div data-tooltip-target="content" 
       class="hidden absolute bottom-full left-1/2 z-20 mb-2 -translate-x-1/2 ...">
    Tooltip content
    <!-- Arrow -->
    <div class="absolute top-full left-1/2 -mt-1 -ml-1 border-4 border-transparent border-t-slate-900"></div>
  </div>
</div>
