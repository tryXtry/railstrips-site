Tabs

Tabs facilitate seamless switching between different views.

Basic

Account Settings

Manage your account details here. You can update your profile picture, email address, and other personal information.

Password Change

Ensure your account is secure by updating your password regularly. Use a strong password with a mix of characters.

Notification Preferences

Choose what updates you want to receive. You can subscribe to our newsletter or get alerts for account activity.

Usage

<div data-controller="tabs"
     data-tabs-active-tab-class-value="border-indigo-500 text-indigo-600"
     data-tabs-inactive-tab-class-value="border-transparent text-slate-500 hover:text-slate-700">
  
  <nav>
    <button data-tabs-target="tab" data-action="click->tabs#change">Tab 1</button>
    <button data-tabs-target="tab" data-action="click->tabs#change">Tab 2</button>
  </nav>

  <div>
     <div data-tabs-target="panel">Content 1</div>
     <div data-tabs-target="panel">Content 2</div>
  </div>

</div>
