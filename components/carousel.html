Carousel

Carousel is a content slider featuring various customization options.

Mountain Landscape

Experience the beauty of nature.

Lakeside View

Quiet moments by the water.

Forest Path

Walk through the ancient woods.

Usage

<div data-controller="carousel" class="relative overflow-hidden">
  <!-- Slides Container -->
  <div data-carousel-target="container" class="flex transition-transform duration-500">
    <div data-carousel-target="slide" class="w-full flex-shrink-0">Slide 1</div>
    <div data-carousel-target="slide" class="w-full flex-shrink-0">Slide 2</div>
  </div>

  <!-- Navigation -->
  <button data-action="click->carousel#previous">Prev</button>
  <button data-action="click->carousel#next">Next</button>

  <!-- Indicators -->
  <button data-carousel-target="indicator" data-action="click->carousel#goTo" data-index="0"></button>
  <button data-carousel-target="indicator" data-action="click->carousel#goTo" data-index="1"></button>
</div>
